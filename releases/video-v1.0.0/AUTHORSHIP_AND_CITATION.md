# Authorship and citation rules

## Citation instructions

Cite the paper when using the method. If you use or adapt the generation files, also cite the specific software release used.

Supplementary software and visualization material accompanying the paper.
Watching or sharing the video does not create a requirement to cite the software.

## Research attribution

The research method and scientific inputs are associated with the paper by
all six authors below. Resource authorship does not change research authorship.

Alejandro G. Yepes, Wessam E. Abdel-Azim, Oscar López, Petros Karamanakos, Ayman S. Abdel-Khalik, and Jesús Doval-Gandoy, “Current References for Minimum Copper Loss and Torque Ripple in the Full Torque-Speed Range for Symmetrical Six-Phase PMSMs With Nonsinusoidal Back-EMF Under an Open-Phase Fault,” in IECON 2026 - 52nd Annual Conference of the IEEE Industrial Electronics Society, Doha, Qatar, 18-21 October 2026. Author preprint: arXiv:2607.24633v3, doi: 10.48550/arXiv.2607.24633.

The original author order was checked against the local manuscript and the
public arXiv record. Cite every author in that order whenever the paper is
referenced. For any other research paper, use its own complete author list
and original order. Do not use "et al." in the prepared full citations.

The paper DOI identifies the author preprint. No IEEE proceedings DOI or
page range has been verified. The software CFF's main authors/title/version
identify the resource; its research-paper entries are references. BibTeX has
a separate sole-author resource entry and two forms of the six-author paper
citation. Select the entry appropriate to the item actually used.
